Position & map alignment

The blue dot comes from your phone's GPS, placed on the Busfest map using an estimated alignment. If it's off, use Fix my position (the crosshair button): stand somewhere you can identify on the map, then tap that spot. Do it at two different places, well apart, and the scale corrects itself too.

No GPS in this viewer?

Some viewers block location without asking. Two ways round it: tap the crosshair and tap where you are, or paste your coordinates from Google Maps (long-press your blue dot, then copy the two numbers shown at the top).

Your van

The van button pins your pitch at your current position. It stays saved on this phone so you can always find your way back.

Compass

The map is drawn north-up (the printed compass rose on it is wrong: Blackmore Park Road is on the right, Hanley Road along the bottom). The compass switches on at your first tap; a blue cone shows which way you're facing and the direction arrow turns with you. If your phone refused it, try again here.
